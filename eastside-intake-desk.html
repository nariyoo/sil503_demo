“Possible amount owed” is a rough estimate. It compares what was paid with the hours × the higher of the stated rate or the minimum wage, plus time-and-a-half over 40 hours when a stub covers one week or less. It does not cover tips, deductions, sick pay or multi-week overtime. Use it to start the conversation, and let legal aid confirm any amount.

      Lawyer only

      “Could filing affect my immigration case?”

      This tool will not answer that question, and advocates shouldn’t either. The answer depends on the person’s full immigration history, any pending applications, and the kind of complaint. A wrong answer in either direction can cause real harm, and only a lawyer or accredited representative can give it.

      What to say

      What to do

      • Don’t guess or reassure. “It should be fine” and “it’s too risky” are both legal advice.
      • Don’t write immigration details into intake notes or the complaint draft unless the lawyer asks.
      • If there is an upcoming immigration hearing, interview or deadline, write down the date and tell legal aid it is urgent.
      • Tell the worker when they should expect to hear from the lawyer, and that they can wait to decide about filing.
